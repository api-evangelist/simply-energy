# Simply Energy (ENGIE)

> Simply Energy is the former brand of the Australian electricity and gas retailer that now
> trades as ENGIE, operated by IPower Pty Ltd and IPower 2 Pty Ltd (ABN 67 269 241 237),
> supplying more than 700,000 residential and business accounts across Victoria, South
> Australia, New South Wales, Queensland and Western Australia. It rebranded from Simply
> Energy to ENGIE in April 2024. Its entire API surface exists because of a statutory mandate:
> it is a designated Consumer Data Right energy data holder (provider number DH002028), listed
> on the live CDR Register under the ENGIE brand. Two surfaces are anonymously usable today -
> generic tariff data (2,452 live ENGIE plans) and CDS discovery status/outages. Everything
> else - usage, billing, invoices, service points, DER - is reachable only by an ACCC
> accredited data recipient acting on explicit consumer consent. There is no developer portal,
> no self-serve API key and no voluntary open data.

No /llms.txt is published on any host in this provider's surface (engie.com.au and
www.simplyenergy.com.au return HTTP 403 to automated fetchers; the two CDR hosts return 404).

## Public APIs (no credentials required)

- [CDR Energy Generic Plans API](https://cdr.energymadeeasy.gov.au/engie/cds-au/v1/energy/plans): 2,452 live ENGIE electricity and gas tariff plans (MARKET and STANDING) across the Ausgrid, Endeavour, Essential Energy, Energex, SA Power Networks, Jemena and AGN distribution zones. Send `x-v: 1`. Plan detail at `/energy/plans/{planId}` with `x-v: 3`. Hosted on the retailer's behalf by the Australian Energy Regulator's Energy Made Easy service.
- [CDR Discovery API](https://public.cdr.engie.com.au/cds-au/v1/discovery/status): CDS Common status and outages, served from the brand's own registered CDR Public Base URI. Send `x-v: 1`. `/discovery/outages` returns scheduled outages.

## Accredited-only API

- CDR Energy Consumer Data API: energy accounts, balances, billing, invoices, concessions, payment schedules, electricity service points, usage and distributed energy resources. No base URL is published anonymously - a data holder's InfoSec and resource base URIs are released only through the authenticated portion of the CDR Register to an accredited data recipient presenting a client certificate.

## What does not exist

- No developer portal. `developer.simplyenergy.com.au`, `api.simplyenergy.com.au`, `docs.simplyenergy.com.au`, `data.simplyenergy.com.au`, `developer.engie.com.au`, `api.engie.com.au`, `docs.engie.com.au` and `data.engie.com.au` do not resolve.
- No self-serve signup, no API keys, no sandbox tier, no partner API.
- No webhooks, events, streaming or AsyncAPI - the CDR energy data-holder contract has no event surface at all.
- No first-party SDK or CLI in any package registry.
- No published SOC 2 / ISO 27001 certification, trust centre or vulnerability disclosure programme.
- No open grid, market or wholesale data. This is a retailer; NEM dispatch and network data sit with AEMO and the distribution businesses.
